Process

From first call to a signed customer

Every campaign runs through the same three phases. Nothing skipped, nothing outsourced to a generic script.

STEP 01

We build the pitch around your business

Before a single rep goes to a door, we sit down with you to understand your offer, your pricing, and what a "good customer" actually looks like for you. From that we write a doorstep script — not a generic sales template, one built for your product.

  • What we cover: pricing, service area, objection handling, what counts as a valid close
  • What you get: a written script + FAQ sheet reps are trained on before day one
  • Typical turnaround: a few days, depending on how complex the offer is
STEP 02

Licensed reps canvass the right neighbourhoods

We route reps to the areas most likely to convert for your specific service, using local knowledge of Winnipeg neighbourhoods rather than a random walk. Every rep on the street carries their own Direct Sellers Act license and identifies themselves as LeadRight WPG, on behalf of your business.

  • Reps work in scheduled blocks, tracked route by route
  • Every rep is a vetted, licensed independent contractor
  • You approve the target areas before canvassing starts
STEP 03

A closed door means a signed agreement

A "sale" isn't a name and a phone number — it's the homeowner signing your service agreement on the spot, with the details set up right there. That's the only thing that counts as a conversion, and it's the only thing you pay commission on.

  • Signed agreements are delivered to you the same day
  • You're billed only for confirmed, signed customers
  • Weekly summary of doors knocked, conversations had, and customers signed
