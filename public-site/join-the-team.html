Join The Team

Straight commission. No ceiling on what you take home.

LeadRight WPG reps are independent contractors, paid per signed customer. No base pay, and no limit on how much you can earn if you can hold a conversation on a doorstep.

The deal

What you need to know before you apply

PAY

100% commission

You're paid a set amount per signed customer, not a wage. No sale means no pay for that door — that's the whole model.

STATUS

Independent contractor

You set your own hours within the campaign's active window. You're not an employee of LeadRight WPG or the client.

LICENSE

Direct Sellers Act rep license

Manitoba requires every individual door-to-door rep to hold their own license through the Consumer Protection Office. We walk you through the application.

A typical day

What the job actually looks like

Morning

Pick up your route and materials

Get your assigned area, the day's script for whichever client you're running, and your license/ID.

Midday

Knock, talk, close

Work the neighbourhood at your own pace. Every closed door means signing the customer up on the spot.

End of day

Log your sales

Submit your signed agreements. Commission is calculated per confirmed, signed customer.

Being straight with you

This isn't a salaried job, and it isn't for everyone

If you need guaranteed weekly pay, this isn't that role. If you're comfortable being paid on results and you're good on a doorstep, this is a straight-commission role with real per-sale payouts and no cap on what you can earn.
